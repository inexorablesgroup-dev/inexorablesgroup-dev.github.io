Our Services

One roof. Every modality.

Whatever your doctor has written on that slip — the machine for it is here, and so is the radiologist who reads it.

MRI

Detailed imaging of brain, spine, joints and abdomen — no radiation. Our staff walk you through the prep and stay in contact through the scan.

CT Scan

Fast, precise cross-sections for head injury, chest, abdomen and stone workups — the scan you need when the answer cannot wait.

Ultrasound (USG)

Whole-abdomen, pelvic and pregnancy scans. Women radiologists are on our team — so a mother can ask for one, and be heard.

X-Ray

Chest, spine and fracture films taken and reported quickly — because a farmer's shoulder and a schoolchild's wrist both hurt today, not next week.

Pathology Lab

Blood work, sugar and thyroid profiles, fever and pregnancy panels — sampled in the same building, so one visit covers everything.

Health Check-up Packages

Full Body, Full Body Master, Pre-Surgical and Antenatal (ANC) profiles — bundled so you know what is being tested before you sit down.

Our Promise

Why doctors send patients here.

MD radiologists read your scan.

Three MD radiologists on our team — and two of them are women, so a mother asking for a female radiologist is not asking for a favour.

Charges told upfront.

You hear the price of the scan or package before it begins, not at the counter afterwards. No surprises, no "extra film charge."

Hygiene taken seriously.

Clean rooms, clean linen, clean needles. A diagnostic centre should lower your pulse, not raise it.

Reports that arrive fast.

Fast, accurate reporting is the whole job. Your treating doctor should be reading your film today — not chasing it next week.

The Signature Rhythm

A day on the Gonda campus.

Structure is a kindness. Exact bell timings are shared at the office — this is the shape of the day.

FIRST BELL

Assembly under the open sky

Prayer, the day’s news read by students, and two minutes of silence before the first period.

MORNING

Core hours — when minds are freshest

Mathematics, science and languages take the morning, in smart classrooms. No child is moved on until the concept lands.

MID-MORNING

Science & computer lab block

Hands on beakers and keyboards. The quietest and the loudest hours of the day, side by side.

MIDDAY

Lunch on the lawns

Tiffins open, friendships form. Teachers eat with their classes — the best conversations happen here.

AFTERNOON

Sport, art & club hour

Coached sport, sketchbooks, debate practice. Every child picks a lane and owns it for the year.

DISPERSAL

Home time — and a message home

Gates open on time. Parents get a WhatsApp update before the child reaches the gate.

Questions Parents Ask

Answers, straight.

Admissions are open from Nursery onwards for the 2026–27 session, and the campus runs through to Class XII. Seats in each section are limited; mid-session transfers are considered case by case.

Collect the prospectus and registration form from the school office against the registration fee, submit the completed form within the given window, and the child and parents may then be called for a short interaction with the Principal. Document verification follows.

For Playgroup to Class I: a photocopy of the birth certificate issued by a competent authority, five passport photographs of the child and parents, the admission form and the Aadhaar card. For higher classes, add the transfer certificate from the previous school; the original birth certificate may be asked for verification.

Parent-teacher meetings, a diary that actually gets checked, and the online parent portal for attendance and circulars — plus WhatsApp updates for results, notices and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from us first.

Fees are accepted through cashless channels only — online payment or MICR cheque. The current fee structure for each class is given with the prospectus at the school office. Call +91 81880 84021 and we will walk you through it.

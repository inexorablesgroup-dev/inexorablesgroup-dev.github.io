Who we are

We believe a child should leave school able to process information, not repeat it. That is the whole method — innovation, creativity and enterprise treated as the syllabus behind the syllabus.

The Jaipuria name has stood in education since 1945. Gonda has carried it since 2018, on one campus that runs from the first day of Nursery to the last board paper of Class XII.

The stages

One campus,five stages.

01 / 05

Early years

Playgroup, Nursery and Kindergarten. Play, language and routine — the years where a child learns how to be at school.

02 / 05

Primary — I to V

Reading, number sense and curiosity. Smart classrooms carry the explaining; the notebook carries the practice.

03 / 05

Middle — VI to VIII

The lab years. Science stops being a chapter and becomes a bench, a beaker and a result to defend.

04 / 05

Secondary — IX to X

Board discipline without board panic. Concepts revisited until they hold, then practised until they are quick.

05 / 05

Senior — XI to XII

Streams, subject choices and the years that set the next decade. The last two years on the same campus as the first.

Admissions 2026–27

Four steps,no guesswork.

STEP 01

Tell us about your child

Send an enquiry — here, or on WhatsApp. Name, the class you are applying for, and a number we can reply on. Nothing else needed yet.

STEP 02

Collect and submit the form

Pick up the prospectus and registration form from the school office against the registration fee, and submit the completed form within the given window.

STEP 03

A short interaction

The child and parents may be called for a brief interaction with the Principal. It is a conversation, not an examination.

STEP 04

Documents and confirmation

Document verification follows, and the seat is confirmed. The office tells you the exact list for your class, so nothing is carried in twice.

Questions parents ask

Answers,straight.

Admissions are open from Nursery onwards for the 2026–27 session, and the campus runs through to Class XII. Seats in each section are limited; mid-session transfers are considered case by case.

Collect the prospectus and registration form from the school office against the registration fee, submit the completed form within the given window, and the child and parents may then be called for a short interaction with the Principal. Document verification follows.

For the early years, broadly: the birth certificate issued by a competent authority, passport photographs of the child and parents, the completed admission form and the Aadhaar card. For higher classes, add the transfer certificate from the previous school. The office confirms the exact list for the class you are applying to, so nothing is carried in twice.

Parent-teacher meetings, a diary that actually gets checked, and the online parent portal for attendance and circulars — plus WhatsApp updates for results, notices and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from us first.

Fees are accepted through cashless channels only — no cash is collected at the counter. The current fee structure for each class, and the payment channels in use this session, are given with the prospectus at the school office. Call +91 81880 84021 and we will walk you through it.

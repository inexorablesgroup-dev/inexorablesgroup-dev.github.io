A Local Mill.
A Real Brand.

Most flour in this district is sold loose, from an unnamed sack. Vardan Staple Foods chose the harder road — mill it here, pack it here, and put our own registered name on the bag.

Wheat comes in from the belt around Gonda. It is cleaned, milled and packed at our Indrapur unit, alongside our besan, sooji and freshly ground haldi, mirch and dhania. One roof, one standard, one name to answer for it.

Grain to Kitchen

Four Steps. Same Every Time.

Every batch walks the same line — which is why one Vardan pack tastes like the last one.

01 — SOURCE

Pick the Grain

Wheat, chana and whole spices bought from the belt around Gonda, checked before anything is unloaded.

02 — CLEAN

Clean & Sift

Stones, dust and light grain taken out first. Nothing goes to the mill until it is clean.

03 — MILL

Mill & Grind

Atta, maida, sooji and besan milled to the fineness each one needs; masale ground on their own line.

04 — PACK

Pack & Dispatch

Sealed in Vardan packs, weighed true, billed with a GST invoice and sent out to your counter.

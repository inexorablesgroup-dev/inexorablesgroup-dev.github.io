The Cold Store Behind the District

A Season's Work.
Kept Safe.

A farmer hands over months of labour at our gate and walks away without it. That is an act of trust, and it is the only thing this business actually sells. Sri Satguru Dev Cold Stores & Ice Factory — registered as Shri Sadgurdev Cold Storage and Ice Plant — has been holding that trust in Gonda for years, chamber after chamber, season after season.

Alongside the cold chambers runs the ice plant, and it does not keep the agricultural calendar. Fish sellers, sweet shops, caterers, transporters and event suppliers come for ice on ordinary Tuesdays in the middle of the year, and it is ready.

Produce goes in as it came. Ice goes out every day. That is the whole promise.

What We Do

Cold Chambers and a Working Ice Plant.

Seasonal Cold Storage

Block Ice

Loading & Handling

Gate to Chamber and Back

Four Steps. Zero Guesswork.

The same order every time, for every depositor — because the whole business rests on the customer knowing exactly what happened to his stock.

01 — BOOK

Reserve Your Space

Tell us the quantity and when you want to bring it in. Space is confirmed against your name before the season fills up.

02 — INTAKE

Weigh & Enter

Your lot is weighed at the gate and entered in the register before it goes into the chamber. You get the record, not a promise.

03 — HOLD

Stored & Watched

Stacked in chamber and held at temperature through the season, with the cooling monitored rather than left to run unattended.

04 — WITHDRAW

Take It Out

Come when your market is right. Your lot is pulled, weighed out against the intake entry and loaded — full lot or part lot.

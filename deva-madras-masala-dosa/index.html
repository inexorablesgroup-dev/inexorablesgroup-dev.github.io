The Shop

One Tawa. One Standard.

A small shop on Station Road that most of Gonda can find without directions. One hot tawa, a short counter, and a queue that builds long before the rush is supposed to start.

The idea has never changed: get the dosa right, get the sambar right, keep the price where a student, a family and a traveller stepping off a train can all say yes. Everything served here is pure vegetarian.

No fuss, no theatre. Crisp edges, hot sambar, fresh coconut chutney — plate after plate, from noon until the last order at night.

What We Serve

Four Ways to Eat Here.

A short menu, done properly — from a single dosa at the counter to a bulk order for the whole office.

Dosa, Off the Tawa

Masala, plain, paneer and cheese — spread thin, crisped to order, folded over hot potato masala and sent out the moment it is ready.

Idli & Vada

Steamed soft, served with sambar and coconut chutney. The lighter order when a full dosa is more than you came for.

Takeaway & Delivery

Packed to travel without going soggy. Send your order ahead on WhatsApp, collect at the counter, skip the wait entirely.

Bulk & Office Orders

Coaching batches, office lunches, family functions — order by the plate for a fixed time slot. Message the count, collect it hot.

Ajmeri Cold Storage and Ice Factory Private Limited was incorporated on 8 June 1976. Half a century later it is still active, still filing, still run by the same family — the oldest registered company in Gonda district.

Four directors, one household. One of them has sat on the board since the year the company was founded. That is not a slogan; it is on the public record at the Registrar of Companies.

When a season's crop is in our chambers, the family's name is on it. That is the whole pitch.

Gate to Chamber

Four Steps. Zero Guesswork.

Every lot moves the same way, in and out — that is how a store stays boring, in the best sense of the word.

01 — BOOK

Reserve Space

Tell us the crop and roughly how much, before harvest. Space is held against your name.

02 — INTAKE

Count & Receipt

Your lot is counted at the gate and entered against your name. You leave with a receipt, not a promise.

03 — STORE

Hold the Cold

Stacked in chamber and kept at temperature through the season, watched daily until you call for it.

04 — WITHDRAW

Call & Collect

Message a day ahead, your lot is pulled and ready at the gate. Counted out against the same record.

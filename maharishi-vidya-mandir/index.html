A Message from the School

Excellence, With Stillness

Maharishi Vidya Mandir has taught the children of Gonda since 1992. Three decades on, the school still runs from the same 2.5 acres at Janki Nagar on Bahraich Road — a campus deliberately set back from the traffic, with room to run and rooms kept quiet.

Three hundred and twelve students are taught here by thirty-seven teachers. That is roughly eight children to a teacher: small enough that a raised hand is answered and a puzzled face is noticed, and small enough that a subject teacher follows a child’s work over years rather than over a single term.

The academic course is the full CBSE syllabus under senior secondary affiliation 2131093. What sits alongside it is Consciousness-Based Education, the approach every Maharishi Vidya Mandir school follows: dedicated Transcendental Meditation and yoga halls on campus, and a daily practice of settling the attention before the lesson begins. The reasoning is plain. A calm mind absorbs more in an hour than a restless one does in three.

Mr. Vinay Kumar Mishra Principal · Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Gonda

Questions Parents Ask

Answers, Straight

Kindergarten through Class XII, subject to seats in each section. We are a CBSE senior secondary school, affiliation number 2131093. For a mid-session transfer, bring the previous school’s report card and speak to the office.

Registration and admission forms are available online and at the school office. Send an enquiry or walk in, collect the form, then complete document verification and fee submission at the counter. The office will tell you exactly what is required for your child’s class.

It is the approach every Maharishi Vidya Mandir school follows: the standard CBSE syllabus, taught alongside a daily practice of Transcendental Meditation and yoga. We have dedicated halls on campus for both. The aim is simple — a child whose attention is settled learns more in the same hour.

The school has 312 students and 37 teaching staff — a ratio of roughly eight children to a teacher. We are deliberately a mid-sized school rather than a crowded one, which is why teachers here know every child by name.

Science and computer laboratories, a library and reading room, music and art rooms, Transcendental Meditation and yoga halls, and sports grounds — all on a pollution-free 2.5-acre campus at Janki Nagar.

The campus is at Janki Nagar on Bahraich Road, Gonda 271003, set back from the traffic. Call the school office on 05262-231698 during working hours to confirm a convenient time, or send a WhatsApp message to 93895 41281 and we will reply with directions.

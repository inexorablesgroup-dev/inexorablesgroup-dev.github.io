Our Story

A hospital built so Gonda wouldn't have to leave.

For too long, a serious case in Gonda meant an ambulance to Lucknow.

Hours on the highway. The wrong hours to lose.

SCPM was built to end that journey.

What began as a memorial hospital for Satish Chandra Pandey has grown, under founder-chairman Dr. O. N. Pandey, into a super-specialty hospital and trauma centre on Station Road.

And alongside it, four colleges — nursing, paramedical, pharmacy and Ayurveda — training the district's next generation of healers.

Departments

One campus. Emergency to recovery.

From a highway accident at 2am to a follow-up dressing three weeks later — the whole path stays in one place.

Trauma & Emergency

The reason the doors never close. Accident and injury cases received round the clock, stabilised first and asked about later.

Critical & Intensive Care

Monitored beds and a team trained for the hours that decide an outcome. The care that used to mean a referral out of the district.

Mother & Child

Women's health, pregnancy care, safe deliveries and newborn care — with the privacy and respect every mother deserves.

Orthopaedics & Surgery

Fractures, joint pain and operative care, with physiotherapy guidance after. Because a farmer's back and a teacher's knee both have to go back to work.

Diagnostics & Pathology

Blood work, sugar profiles and routine screening on site — so the test, the result and the doctor reading it are all in one building.

SCPM Colleges

Nursing, paramedical, pharmacy and Ayurveda — four institutions on the Lucknow Road campus at Haripur, training healers where they are needed.

The Developer

Land You Can Build On.

Buying land in Gonda usually means unclear papers, uncertain boundaries and a road that never arrives. Archa Infrastructure and Developers Pvt Ltd built Gonard City to remove all three.

Spread across 45,915 sq m at Rajapur / Mathura Chaubey on Lucknow Road, Gonard City is a planned, gated township registered with UP RERA under UPRERAPRJ53857 — residential plots from about 1,200 to 2,500 sq ft, and commercial plots from about 3,500 to 4,000 sq ft.

Registered paperwork, laid-out roads, and a layout plan you can see before you pay.

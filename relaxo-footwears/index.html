The Showroom

One of Gonda's Few Brand Showrooms.

Most footwear in this city is bought loose, off a market table, with no box and no answer if the sole gives way. This is a Relaxo exclusive outlet — listed on the company's own store network, stocking only genuine Relaxo, Sparx, Flite and Bahamas ranges.

That means the size you ask for is a real size, the price on the box is the price you pay, and what you carry home is the same product Relaxo sells anywhere in India. No copies. No bargaining theatre.

Twelve hours a day, seven days a week. Court Road stays open when the rest of the market shuts.

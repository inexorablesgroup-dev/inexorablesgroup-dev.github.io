The Gonda Showroom

A Gorakhpur house, now on Station Road.

Aisshpra Gems & Jewels is an eastern-UP jewellery house built by the Saraf family in Gorakhpur. Gonda is its 15th showroom — opened in April 2026 and inaugurated by MLA Prateek Bhushan Singh alongside the brand's directors.

It is run here by local franchise partners — Ravi Modi, Tushar Agarwal, Vipul Modi and Shalabh Modi — so the buying power and design studio of a chain sit behind a counter staffed by people from this city.

Gold, diamond, polki and silver under one roof, with the brand's newer Jewel Story line of lightweight natural-diamond pieces in 9KT, 14KT and 18KT.

Collections

Six counters. One standard.

The full Aisshpra portfolio, now on the Gonda floor — hallmarked gold, certified diamonds, polki and silver.

Bridal Gold

Full wedding suites — necklace, jhumka, maang tikka, chooda — from the chain's bridal studio, shown here in Gonda.

Everyday Gold

Chains, bangles, kadas and rings in hallmarked gold — the pieces a family buys between weddings, not only for them.

Diamonds & Polki

Certified diamond jewellery and uncut polki — stones shown under light, with certification, before anything is billed.

Silver

Jewel Story Diamonds

Gifting & Coins

Hallmarked coins, nazrana pieces and light daily wear — ready-boxed for weddings, births and Dhanteras.

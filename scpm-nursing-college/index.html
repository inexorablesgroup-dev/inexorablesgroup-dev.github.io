The Institution

An education in the health sciences, taught beside a working hospital.

SCPM Group of Hospital & Education is a health-science group in Gonda, Uttar Pradesh. Four institutes stand on one campus on Lucknow Road, Haripur — the College of Nursing, the institute for paramedical and allied health sciences, the Institute of Pharmacy and the Institute of Ayurveda. Together they publish thirty programmes at undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma level.

The nursing and allied-health programmes are affiliated to Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Avadh University, Ayodhya, and are stated as approved by the Indian Nursing Council and the U.P. State Medical Faculty. Eligibility and recognition for any individual course are confirmed in writing at the time of counselling, not promised on a webpage.

What separates the group from a standalone college is the second address. SCPM Super Specialty Hospital & Trauma Center stands on Station Road in Gonda town, under the same management. Clinical posting is therefore not an arrangement negotiated with an unrelated institution each session; it happens inside the organisation that admitted the student. For a family in Gonda, Balrampur or Bahraich, that is the difference between a certificate and a trained practitioner.

The group is directed by a named family of officers — a Founder Chairman, a President and two Managing Directors — and its stated purpose is unchanged since the first batch: shaping future healers with knowledge, skill and humanity.

Faculty & Mentorship

Taught by people who still work the wards.

A student who has only read about a patient has not yet met one. Our teaching day ends where the ward round begins.

The teaching principle of the group

Because the hospital and the college belong to the same organisation, the people who supervise a clinical posting are colleagues of the people who set the examination. A doubt raised on the ward can be settled in the classroom the following morning, and a weakness noticed in the classroom can be corrected on the ward the same week. That short distance between theory and practice is the whole argument for studying here rather than at a college that must borrow its clinical hours from somewhere else.

No individual faculty profiles are published on this concept page. The admissions desk introduces the teaching staff of the relevant institute during a campus visit.

Admissions

Four steps from enquiry to admission.

The college office deals with families directly. There is no agent between a student and a seat.

1

Send an enquiry

A name, the institute or course under consideration, and a WhatsApp number. That is the whole of what is needed to begin.

2

Receive the course details in writing

Eligibility, duration and the fee structure for the exact programme named — sent to the number given, so that the family can read it together before deciding anything.

3

Visit the campus and be counselled

Lucknow Road, Haripur, Monday to Saturday. Marksheets are verified, eligibility is checked against university and council norms, and the choice of stream is discussed honestly before any payment is made.

4

Confirm the seat, then begin clinical posting

Admission is completed and hostel accommodation arranged where seats allow. Teaching runs on campus; the clinical hours are served at SCPM Super Specialty Hospital & Trauma Center.

The Outlet

One Cup. The Whole Evening.

Chai Sutta Bar came to Gonda with a simple idea — good chai, served in a clay kulhad, in a room where nobody rushes you out.

The chain started in Indore in 2016 and now runs 650-plus outlets across the country. This one is Gonda's own — the same kulhad, the same menu, run by people from here who know which table the regulars want.

Students after class, families on a Sunday, friends who came for one cup and ordered garlic bread twice. That is a normal evening here — and the reason this outlet is ranked #1 of 124 cafes in Gonda.

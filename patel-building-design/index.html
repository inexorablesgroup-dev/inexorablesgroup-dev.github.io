How We Build

Design. Approve. Build.

The same order every time — whether it's a naksha alone or a full turnkey house.

01 — DESIGN

We Draw It First.

We visit the plot, understand the family's needs and the budget, and put it on paper — plan, elevation and structure — before anyone touches a brick.

02 — APPROVE

You Sign Off.

Drawings, material specification and a written estimate — all agreed with you before work begins. Changes are cheap on paper and expensive in concrete.

03 — BUILD

We Execute It.

Our own team on site, stage by stage — foundation, slab, plaster, finishing — with progress shown to you at every stage until handover.

Owned by the Farmers It Serves

Registered in 2019.
Rooted in This Belt.

Athirst Farmer Producer Company Limited was incorporated in April 2019 at Shri Pal Purwa on Lova Belsar Road, Gonda — not as a trading firm, but as a company the farmers of this belt own themselves.

Seven local farmer-directors sit on the board. Every decision — what rate to hold, which buyer to sell to, which input to stock — is taken by people who farm this soil, not by an office elsewhere.

A bank-financed FPO, not a paper one. Our working capital is backed by scheduled-bank credit, which means we can pay members when the crop moves — not after the buyer pays us.

What the Company Does

From the Field to the Buyer.

Six things the company does for the farmers who own it — each one worth more done together than alone.

Crop Aggregation

Members' produce is pooled into lots large enough to be worth a serious buyer's time — and a serious buyer's rate.

Bulk Input Supply

Seed, fertiliser and crop protection bought at company scale and passed to members — not at retail counter prices.

Hold, Don't Distress-Sell

Aggregation lets the company wait out a bad week instead of forcing every member to sell on harvest day.

Buyer & Mandi Linkage

We take the pooled lot to traders, processors and institutional buyers — and negotiate as a company, not as a queue of farmers.

Credit & Working Capital

Bank-backed working capital sits behind the company, so members can be paid when the produce moves.

Advisory & Scheme Access

Sowing guidance, input planning and help reaching the government schemes an individual farmer rarely gets to on their own.

How Membership Works

Four Steps. No Middleman.

The same path for every member, whether you bring one quintal or fifty.

01 — JOIN

Become a Member

Register with your land details and crop. Shareholding in the company follows — you become an owner, not a customer.

02 — PLAN

Sow With Support

Inputs are ordered together ahead of the season, so you are not paying peak retail in sowing week.

03 — POOL

Bring the Harvest

Your produce is weighed, recorded against your name, and pooled with the rest into a saleable lot.

04 — SETTLE

Sell & Get Paid

The company sells the pooled lot and settles each member against the recorded weight — one rate, openly stated.

Our Promise

The Forecourt Standard.

Every customer at this pump has these rights. Ask for any of them, any time — no explanation needed.

Customer Charter Applies to every fill
S-01

Zero on the meter, every time

Watch the display return to zero before the nozzle goes in. Ask the attendant to show you — it is your right.

S-02

Quantity check on demand

Ask for the five-litre measure and we will run it in front of you. No questions, no delay.

S-03

Quality check on demand

Filter-paper and density tests are available at the outlet. Ask, and the reading is shown to you.

S-04

A printed bill for every fill

Ask and you get a receipt — litres, rate and amount. Essential if you are claiming it or running a fleet.

S-05

Company fuel, company rate

Bharat Petroleum product at the notified pump rate. What the board says is what the meter charges.

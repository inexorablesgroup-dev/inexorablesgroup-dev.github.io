Our House

A jeweller Gonda walks in trusting.

Some shops sell ornaments. Vandana Jewellers sells certainty — a fair weight, a rate written where everyone can read it, and a counter you can come back to.

We stand where Gonda has always bought its gold: Bharat Milap Chouraha, in the Chowk bazaar. Ornaments are weighed in front of you, purity is shown before it is sold, and making charges are said out loud before the bill is written.

From a child's first earrings to a daughter's full bridal set, families return here for one reason: nothing is hidden.

Collections

Six collections. One standard.

Everything below is weighed before your eyes, shown for purity, and billed openly.

Bridal Sets

Full wedding suites — necklace, jhumka, maang tikka, chooda — composed to be photographed and kept.

Gold — 22K & 18K

Chains, bangles, kadas and coins in 916 and 750 gold. The rate board decides the price, not the salesman.

Diamonds & Polki

Solitaires and uncut polki — every stone shown to you under light, never hurried across the counter.

Silver & Payal

Payals, utensils, pooja articles and gifting silver — weighed and stamped, from everyday to heirloom.

Temple & Antique

Nakshi and temple work in deep matte gold — pieces that look inherited the day they are bought.

Gifting & Coins

Hallmarked coins, nazrana pieces and light daily-wear — ready-boxed for weddings, births and Dhanteras.

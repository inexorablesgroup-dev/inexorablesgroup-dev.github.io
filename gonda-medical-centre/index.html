Our Story

Care that doesn't make you travel for it.

For a difficult delivery, a keyhole surgery, or a fertility treatment — the answer in Gonda used to be Lucknow.

Hours on the road. Days off work. Money spent before a single test was done.

Gonda Medical Centre was built to end that journey.

Maternity, surgery, laparoscopy and IVF — practised here at Patel Nagar, by doctors this district already knows, with a chemist counter in the same building.

Our Services

Specialist care, on your doorstep.

From a first pregnancy to a keyhole procedure to a long-awaited baby — the work happens here, in Gonda.

Maternity & Obstetrics

Antenatal checkups, safe delivery and after-care. From the first scan to the first cry — one team, one file, one place you already trust.

IVF & Fertility

Infertility investigation and IVF treatment — one of the very few places in Gonda offering it. Explained plainly, handled privately, without a trip to the city.

Gynaecology

Women's health across every stage — irregular cycles, pain, menopause and routine screening, discussed with a lady doctor and complete privacy.

Laparoscopic Surgery

Keyhole procedures instead of long incisions — smaller scars, less pain, and a patient back on their feet in days rather than weeks.

General Surgery

Planned and urgent operations handled in-house, with the same doctors seeing you before the surgery and after it — not a stranger at each visit.

Chemist Counter

A medical store in the same building. Your prescription is filled before you leave — no second stop at the bazaar with a patient in the car.

Why families choose us.

Honest advice, always.

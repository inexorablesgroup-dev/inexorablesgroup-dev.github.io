A message from the college

Higher education,
close to home.

Nandini Nagar Mahavidyalaya was founded at Nawabganj so that a student from this district would not have to leave it to earn a real degree. Three decades later the same idea holds the campus together: arts to agriculture, commerce to law, taught where the students already live.

Eight faculties sit under one management, and a single administration answers for all of them. The laboratories, the library and the grounds are used rather than displayed — and the degree at the end of it is awarded by the university, not by us.

“A rural campus, held to an urban syllabus.”

Students arrive from villages and small towns across Gonda district. What they need from a college is plain enough: a programme that is genuinely running, a teacher who turns up, and an office that answers when they ask a question. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Life at Nandini Nagar

Eight faculties. One campus.

Undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across the sciences, the humanities, and the professional and vocational streams — all at Nawabganj, under one administration.

Academics

B.A., B.Sc., B.Com. and B.B.A. at the undergraduate level; M.A., M.Sc. and M.Com. above them. The academic backbone of the mahavidyalaya since its founding.

Agriculture

A flagship faculty: the agriculture degree alongside M.Sc. Agriculture with specialisations, taught in a district that lives by its land.

Law & Pharmacy

LL.B. at Nandini Nagar Vidhi Mahavidyalaya, and a pharmacy diploma approved by the Pharmacy Council of India — professional routes on the same campus.

Sports & Physical Education

B.P.Ed. alongside the college sports grounds — the programme that puts trained coaches and physical-education teachers into schools across the district.

Service & Placement

NSS and NCC units for the character built alongside a degree, and a Training & Placement Cell for the years that follow it.

Admissions

From enquiry to enrolment.

No agents, no guesswork. Four steps from the first message to the first class — and one trip to the campus, not three.

I

Send your enquiry

Your name, the programme you are aiming for, and a mobile number. That is enough to open a file — WhatsApp works as well as the form.

II

Eligibility confirmed

The faculty office checks your marks against the university norms and tells you plainly whether the programme is open to you.

III

Documents & verification

Marksheets, transfer certificate, identity proof, photographs. You get the full list before you travel, and originals are checked at the counter.

IV

Fee & enrolment

The fee is recorded against your name and the receipt issued the same visit. University registration and class allotment follow.

One form. Eight faculties. The right desk.

Today a student has to already know which admission portal applies to them. Here a single enquiry is routed to the faculty office that can actually answer it — and the reply comes back on WhatsApp, in Hindi or English.

  • Eligibility and document list sent back on WhatsApp
  • Examination notices, results and fee due-dates pushed to enrolled students instead of posted as a PDF

Questions students ask

Answers, straight.

Eight faculties: arts, science, commerce, agriculture, education, physical education, law and computer science — at both bachelor’s and master’s level, along with a pharmacy diploma approved by the Pharmacy Council of India. Seats in each programme are limited by the university’s sanctioned intake.

Send an enquiry or visit the college office at Nawabganj, get your eligibility confirmed against the university norms, then complete document verification and fee submission at the counter. The four steps are laid out in full above.

Nandini Nagar Mahavidyalaya is affiliated to Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur, having earlier been affiliated to Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Avadh University. Your degree is awarded by the university, not by the college.

Inside the Lab

Standards you can't see, results you can.

NABL accreditation, not a claim on a board.

NABL is India's national accreditation body for testing laboratories. It means the equipment is calibrated, the methods are documented, the reagents are tracked and the results are auditable — checked by someone other than us.

For a patient in Gonda, that is the difference between a number you repeat at three labs and a number your doctor trusts the first time.

  • Standardised processing through the Pathkind network
  • Barcoded samples — your name never leaves your tube
  • Reports formatted with reference ranges your doctor reads daily
  • Repeat testing on any result that looks out of pattern

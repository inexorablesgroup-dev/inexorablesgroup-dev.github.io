Our Story

Care that doesn't make you travel for it.

For too long, a specialist opinion meant a ticket to Lucknow.

Hours on the road. Days off work. A family waiting at home.

R.B. Rao Memorial was built to end that journey.

Nine specialities, one building, one team — orthopaedics, gynaecology, IVF, cardiology, neurology, general surgery, internal medicine, plastic surgery and paediatrics.

Best outcomes for every patient, every time. Here in Gonda, minutes from your door.

Specialities

Nine specialities. One roof.

From a child's fever to a grandmother's knee replacement — walk in once, and let us take it from there.

Cardiology

Chest pain, blood pressure, ECG and heart follow-ups — assessed here, so a cardiac worry no longer means a drive to Lucknow.

Paediatrics & Internal Medicine

Gentle hands for small patients, thorough care for adults. Fevers, infections, diabetes, vaccinations — answered with patience.

Gynaecology & IVF

Women's health, pregnancy care, safe deliveries and fertility treatment — with the privacy and respect every family deserves.

Orthopaedics

Joint pain, fractures and rehabilitation guidance. Because a farmer's back and a teacher's knee both deserve to work painlessly.

Neurology

Headaches, seizures, stroke follow-up, nerve and spine complaints — reviewed by a specialist without leaving the district.

General & Plastic Surgery

Planned and urgent surgical care, plus reconstructive and cosmetic procedures — explained fully before anything is decided.

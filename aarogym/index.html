The Club

Built on One Idea: Everyone Belongs.

Most gyms are built for people who are already fit. Aarogym was built for whoever walks through the door — the first-timer who has never touched a barbell, and the athlete chasing a number.

Two branches in Gonda: the flagship at Civil Lines and our Maharajganj branch on Station Road. Both air-conditioned, both fully equipped, both staffed by trainers who correct your form instead of watching their phone.

Strength, cardio, flexibility and mindfulness — the whole picture, under one roof.

What You Get

  • Air-conditioned halls, premium free weights and machines.
  • A dedicated yoga & Zumba studio — and a real boxing ring.
  • A trainer with you on the floor, not just on induction day.
  • Clean locker rooms, showers, and nutrition guidance included.

You don't have to be fit to walk in. You walk in to get fit.

How It Works

Three Steps. No Guesswork.

The same start for everyone — day one beginner or ten-year lifter.

01 — ASSESS

Come In. Try It.

A free trial session and a sit-down with a trainer. Your goal, your injuries, your schedule — noted before you lift anything.

02 — PLAN

We Build Your Programme.

Training split, class timings and diet guidance written for your life — not copied off a poster. Pick the plan that fits; nothing is oversold.

03 — PROGRESS

We Keep You Going.

Form checked, weights reviewed, plan adjusted as you get stronger. The hard part is showing up — we handle the rest.

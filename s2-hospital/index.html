Our Story

Care that doesn't make you travel for it.

For too long, serious treatment in Gonda meant a ticket to Lucknow.

Hours on the road. Days off work. A family waiting at home.

S2 was built to end that journey.

Established in 2023 by the Pandey family as a full private-limited hospital — not a one-room clinic — S2 brings multiple specialities, diagnostics and admission beds together at Awas Vikas Colony, Civil Lines.

Modern equipment. Honest advice. All of it here, minutes from your door.

Specialities

One roof. Every need.

From a child's fever to a grandmother's knee — walk in once, and let us take it from there. Call ahead to confirm which specialist is consulting today.

General Medicine

Fevers, infections, blood pressure, diabetes. The everyday medicine a family leans on — done thoroughly, not hurriedly.

Paediatrics

Gentle hands for small patients. Growth checks, vaccinations and late-night worries — answered with patience.

Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Women's health, pregnancy care and safe deliveries — with the privacy and respect every mother deserves.

Orthopaedics

Joint pain, fractures and physiotherapy guidance. Because a farmer's back and a teacher's knee both deserve to work painlessly.

Diagnostics & Pathology

Blood work, sugar profiles and routine screening on site. The lab is down the corridor, not across town.

Admission & Inpatient Care

Beds, nursing and observation for patients who need to stay. Help first, formalities later.

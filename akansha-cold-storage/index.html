When a farmer hands over his crop in April, he is handing over the whole year. M/S Akansha Enterprises exists to give that back to him in December — same bags, same count, sound condition.

The store stands at Village Umaria in Tarabganj, and carries the largest licensed cold storage capacity listed for Gonda district. Big enough to take the block's harvest; close enough that the trolley reaches the gate the same morning it leaves the field.

Four Steps. Zero Guesswork.

Every lot moves the same way, in and out. That is how a season passes without a single argument over bags.

01 — BOOK

Book Your Space

Tell us your quantity before the intake window. Space is confirmed against the season's rate.

02 — INTAKE

Weigh & Record

Bags counted and weighed at the gate, entered against your name and lot number, receipt in your hand.

03 — STORE

Stack & Hold

Your lot stacked in its own place and held at storage temperature, watched through the season.

04 — WITHDRAW

Draw & Settle

Take out the whole lot or part of it whenever the market suits you. Bags counted out, rent settled, done.

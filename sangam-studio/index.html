The Studio

A Wedding Happens Once.

The music, the nervous laugh before the phere, the moment your mother finally sits down. It all passes in a single day. Sangam Studio exists to make sure none of it passes unseen.

Two decades and more of Gonda weddings have taught us where to stand and when to stay quiet. We shoot candid when the moment is real, cinematic when the frame deserves it, and traditional when the family wants every face on record — often all three at the same wedding.

From Gonda to Lucknow to a pre-wedding shoot in the hills, the camera travels with you.

What You Get

  • Your date held the moment you book it. No double bookings.
  • Package and price agreed before the first shot. Nothing added later.
  • Candid, cinematic or traditional — you choose the style, we plan around it.
  • Edited films and printed albums, delivered — not left half-finished.

How We Work

Three Steps. Zero Panic.

The same calm process, whether it is a one-day roka or a five-day wedding.

01 — THE SITTING

We Meet First.

Tell us the dates, the venues and how your family likes to be photographed. You see our work, we agree the style and the package — before anything is booked.

02 — THE DAY

We Stay Out of the Way.

Our team arrives before the guests and works to a plan you already approved. Nobody is pulled out of a ritual to pose. You attend your own wedding.

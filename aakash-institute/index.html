NEET (UG) for medical, JEE Main and Advanced for engineering, Foundation programmes for Classes 8 to 12, and preparation for NTSE, KVPY and Olympiads. Which batch is currently running for a given class is best confirmed at the counter.

Send an enquiry or message on WhatsApp, take a counselling call to fix the right course, visit the centre, then complete document verification and fee payment. Cash, credit or debit card and online payment are all accepted here.

ANTHE is Aakash’s national scholarship examination, conducted at this centre. It offers fee scholarships of up to 100% along with cash awards, on merit. Registration windows open each year — ask the counter for the current dates before you enrol.

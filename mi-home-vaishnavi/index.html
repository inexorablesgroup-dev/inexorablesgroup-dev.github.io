Before you come in

Questions? Answers.

Yes. Mi Home is Xiaomi's own brand-store format, and this Gonda outlet is operated as a franchise by Vaishnavi Infotech. Every device is official stock, sold with a proper bill and the full manufacturer warranty.

An authorised Mi service centre runs from this same address, so warranty and out-of-warranty jobs are handled in Gonda rather than sent away. Bring the handset and the bill, and you will be given a job number.

Message the job number to us on WhatsApp instead of calling. That is the whole idea behind the assistant demonstrated above — you get the status back without waiting for someone at the counter to be free.

Exchange and finance schemes run through the year and change with the brand's current offers. Send us the model of your old handset on WhatsApp and we will tell you what it is worth against a new one today.

Ask first. A quick WhatsApp message with the model name saves you the ride in from Karnailganj, Colonelganj or anywhere else in the district if the variant or colour you want is not on the shelf.

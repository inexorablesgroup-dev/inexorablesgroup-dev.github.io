Courses Offered

From first year to post-graduation.

Undergraduate, postgraduate and professional courses, affiliated to Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur and Dr. R.M.L. Avadh University, Ayodhya.

Graduation — B.A., B.Sc., B.Com

The three main degree streams, taught to the university syllabus with regular classes and internal assessment. Semester admissions each session.

Post-Graduation

Master’s programmes for students who want to go further without leaving the district — taught on the same campus, by the same faculty who know them.

B.Ed — Teacher Training

The professional course that turns a graduate into a teacher. Limited seats, admitted through the prescribed state process.

B.P.Ed — Physical Education

For students headed towards sports, coaching and physical-training posts. Open grounds on campus, not a classroom-only course.

Library, ICT Lab & Campus

A computerised office and library, an ICT lab for students, WiFi and CCTV across the campus — the basics done properly.

NCC, NSS & Sports

NCC and NSS units, sports grounds, and medical camps held on campus. Where a student learns what a certificate cannot record.

How Admission Works

From enquiry to first class.

Six clear steps. No agent, no confusion — the office will tell you exactly where you stand at each one.

STEP 01

Send an enquiry

Message the college on WhatsApp or fill the short form below. Tell us the course you want — UG, PG, B.Ed or B.P.Ed.

STEP 02

Check eligibility & fees

The office confirms the marks and documents your course needs, and shares the fee structure for the current session.

STEP 03

Online registration

Complete the registration form for the session. The help desk in the office sorts out any technical trouble on the spot.

STEP 04

Document verification

Bring your marksheets, transfer certificate and ID to the counter. Originals are checked and returned the same visit.

STEP 05

Fee submission

Pay the session fee at the office and collect the receipt. Your name enters the roll of the college that day.

STEP 06

Classes begin — and updates follow

Timetable, exam dates, practical schedules and result notices reach you on WhatsApp instead of a notice board you have to travel to read.

Questions Students Ask

Answers, straight.

Graduation in B.A., B.Sc. and B.Com, post-graduation courses, and the professional courses B.Ed and B.P.Ed. Some diploma and certificate courses also run. Seats in each course are limited by the sanctioned intake.

Send an enquiry or come to the office, complete the online registration for the session, then bring your marksheets and certificates for verification and submit the fee. B.Ed and B.P.Ed follow the admission process prescribed by the state and the university.

The college is affiliated to Maa Pateshwari (Devipatan) University, Balrampur, and Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Avadh University, Ayodhya, depending on the course. The office will confirm the affiliating university for the course you are applying to.

Degrees for past batches are distributed from the college office — bring your ID and enrolment details. Message the office number and we will tell you whether your degree has arrived before you make the trip.

At Babhnan, near the Babhnan sugar mill, in Gonda district (PIN 271313) — on the main road, easy to reach from the Babhnan railway station and by bus from Gonda and Basti side. Use the Get Directions button above.

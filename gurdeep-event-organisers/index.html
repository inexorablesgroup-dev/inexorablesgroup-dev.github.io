The Team

One Family. One Standard.

A wedding is one day for you and a hundred small decisions for us — the tent, the stage, the flowers, the kitchen, the timing of every course. Gurdeep Event Organisers exists so that none of it becomes your problem.

We are a family-run outfit from Malviya Nagar, Civil Line, with our own trained staff — an operations team on site and our own kitchen crew, not contractors hired the night before. That is the difference you taste in the food and see in the timing.

From an intimate birthday at home to a full wedding at a Gonda lawn or banquet hall, the same people plan it and the same people run it on the day.

What You Can Count On

How We Work

Three Steps. Zero Panic.

The same calm process, whether it's a birthday at home or a three-day wedding.

01 — SIT DOWN

Date, Guests, Budget.

One sitting to fix the three things everything else depends on — your date, how many people, and what you want to spend. We tell you honestly what that budget can do.

02 — PLAN

We Lock It In.

Menu finalised, decor decided, venue coordinated, staff assigned. Written package, agreed rate, your date blocked in our calendar — and it stays yours.

03 — EVENT DAY

You Enjoy It.

Our team arrives early, sets up, serves and clears. You greet your guests instead of chasing the tent-wala. That is the whole point.

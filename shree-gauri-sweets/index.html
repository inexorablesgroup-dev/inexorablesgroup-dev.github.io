Our Story

One Counter. One Standard.

In Gonda, sweets are how families speak. A wedding, a result, a new shop, a festival morning — it starts with a box from the counter.

That is the work here on Utraula Road. Khoya sweets worked in our own kitchen, tray by tray. Chaat fried to order at the counter. Boxes packed so they arrive looking the way they left.

Nearly four hundred reviews and a 4.1 rating say the city agrees. The counter opens at nine and does not slow down until ten.

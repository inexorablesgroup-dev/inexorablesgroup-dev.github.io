Who we are

No family should have to send a child away to find a school of this standard.

So one was built here — on twenty-five acres, in Gonda.

The doors opened on 2 April 2017. The intent has not moved since: a CISCE curriculum taught the way it was written to be taught — ICSE to Class X, ISC to Class XII, English medium throughout, one unbroken journey from Nursery.

Twenty-five acres means learning is never confined to a classroom. Laboratories, a library, creative studios, a sports complex and a swimming pool sit on the same ground the children cross every morning.

Families choose the shape — day, day-cum-boarding, or full residential — and the standard stays the same in all three. Boarders live in air-conditioned rooms shared by six, eat from an in-house kitchen, and speak to home on a weekly call schedule.

Yashmay World School is Gonda district's only CISCE school. That is a responsibility before it is a distinction.

Academics

One journey, five stages.

01

Early Years

Nursery – Class II

Language, number sense and the habit of asking. Small routines, a lot of play, and a first year that makes school feel like somewhere a child wants to be.

02

Primary

Class III – V

Reading for meaning, writing at length, and the first laboratory sessions. Clubs and the studios open up; every child finds something that is theirs.

03

Middle

Class VI – VIII

Subjects separate out and the practicals get serious. This is the stage where study habits are built — long before the boards make them urgent.

04

ICSE Years

Class IX – X

Two years pointed at the Class X ICSE board. The council's paper rewards understanding over recall, so that is what the teaching is built around.

05

ISC Years

Class XI – XII

Streams chosen, ISC ahead, and the university question in the room. Teachers here know each student by name well before this stage begins.

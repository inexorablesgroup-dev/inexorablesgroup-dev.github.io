Our Story I

A full school — not a stopping point.

Since 2001, St. Xavier’s Senior Secondary School has taught the whole ladder on one campus at Pant Nagar — nursery, primary, middle, secondary and senior secondary, under a single standard and a single staff room. Many schools in the district end at Class VIII or Class X. Ours does not.

That continuity is the point. A child admitted in Nursery leaves with a CBSE Class XII result and no anxious transfer year in between — no new uniform at fourteen, no starting again among strangers. Teachers who taught a student in Class VI still know that student in Class XII, and the stream conversation in Class XI happens with people who have watched the child grow up.

Admit once. Finish here. Fourteen years of schooling, taught in order, on one campus at Pant Nagar. St. Xavier’s Senior Secondary School · Gonda

Joining Us IV

Four steps, in the order they happen.

Nothing here needs a document on the first day. It begins with one enquiry and a conversation.

01

Send an enquiry

Tell us the child’s name and the class you are considering. The admissions desk replies on WhatsApp — in Hindi or English, whichever is easier.

02

Visit the campus

Ten minutes on the grounds tells you more than any website. Walk in on a working day, or call ahead and the office will keep time aside.

03

Bring the records

The previous school’s report card and transfer certificate, where the child is joining mid-ladder. The office will tell you exactly where the child fits.

04

Confirm the seat

Section strengths are finite, so seats are confirmed in the order enquiries are completed. You will be told the current position for that class plainly.

Questions Parents Ask V

Answers, plainly given.

The Central Board of Secondary Education — CBSE affiliation number 2130660, as a senior secondary, co-educational school. That means the national syllabus, the CBSE Class X and Class XII board examinations, and a certificate recognised anywhere in the country.

Nursery through Class XII, subject to seats in each section. Mid-session transfers are considered case by case — bring the previous school’s report card and transfer certificate when you visit, and the office will tell you where the child fits.

On the Gonda–Lucknow Marg at Pant Nagar, in revenue village Pared Sarkar — on the main Lucknow road rather than inside the town congestion. Use the directions link below, or call the office and someone will talk you in from the nearest landmark.

Parent–teacher meetings through the year, a diary that actually gets checked, and WhatsApp updates for results, circulars and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from the school first — not from another parent.

School and office hours shift between the summer and winter sessions, as they do at every school in the district. Call the office on 05262 230731, or message +91 89483 29000, for the current timings before you visit.

St. Xavier’s has been serving families in Gonda since 2001, and is listed in the CBSE school directory as a senior secondary, co-educational institution at Village Pared Sarkar, Gonda.

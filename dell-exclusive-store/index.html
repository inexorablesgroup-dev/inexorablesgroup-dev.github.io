Questions? Answers.

Yes — this is a Dell Exclusive Store, an authorised brand outlet, and the only one of its kind in Gonda district. Every machine is original stock with a proper invoice and manufacturer warranty.

The store operates as an authorised Dell service point, so warranty and out-of-warranty work is handled here regardless of where the machine was purchased. Call before you come so the right technician is at the counter.

Tell us the budget and what it is actually for — notes and video lectures, Tally and billing, design work, or gaming. The specification that matters changes completely between those, and we would rather sell you the right machine once.

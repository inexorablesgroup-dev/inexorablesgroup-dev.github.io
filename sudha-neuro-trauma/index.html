Our Story

A head injury cannot wait for Lucknow.

An accident on the highway. A sudden weakness down one side. A fall that changes everything.

For years, those families were sent down the road to a city three hours away — with the clock running.

Sudha Neuro And Trauma Center was built so that journey isn't the first thing you have to do.

Neuro and trauma care on Circular Road, with Sky Diagnostic Centre's scans and lab work under the same roof — so the diagnosis starts the moment you arrive, not after another ambulance ride.

Consultation, imaging and admission — decided in one place, by one team.
Our Services

Neurology

Stroke, seizures, persistent headache, weakness and nerve pain. Neurological complaints assessed properly here, instead of being referred out on sight.

Trauma & Accident Care

Road accidents, head injury, falls and fractures. Stabilise first, paperwork after — with imaging available in the same corridor.

Spine & Orthopaedics

Back and neck pain, slipped disc, joint and bone injury. Because a farmer's spine and a driver's shoulder both have to go back to work.

General Medicine

Fever, infection, blood pressure and diabetes — the everyday medicine a family leans on, and the follow-up care that keeps a discharged patient well.

Your Visit

From door to diagnosis, without the wait.

02

Our Promise

Why families choose us.

Honest advice, always.

If a scan isn't needed, we'll say so. If a case belongs at a higher centre, we'll say that too — and send you with your reports in hand, not empty-handed.

Prices on paper.

Consultation, scan and admission charges are explained before anything begins. Ayushman Bharat cases are handled as cashless — ask at the desk.

One roof, one file.

Doctor, imaging, lab and pharmacy on the same premises, with backup power. Nobody is sent out into the road holding a slip.

Here for the long run.

Your file, your scans, your follow-ups — kept and remembered. Neuro and spine recovery is measured in months, and we stay through them.

The family is treated too.

A neuro or trauma admission is frightening for everyone standing in the corridor. The plan is explained in plain Hindi, the costs are said out loud before anything begins, and the questions keep getting answered after the first one.

For patients who cannot travel back for a test, Sky Diagnostic collects the sample at home — and the report reaches the phone instead of a counter.

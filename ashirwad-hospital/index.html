Our Story

A delivery room that shouldn't be four hours away.

When labour starts at midnight, distance is the enemy.

For too many families in Gonda, a caesarean once meant a night on the road to Lucknow.

Ashirwad was built so that journey is never necessary.

A maternity and surgical centre on Station Road, led by Dr. Alok Agarwal — trusted by Gonda families for more than fifteen years.

Our Care

Motherhood and surgery, under one roof.

From the first scan to the day you walk home with your baby — and for the operations a family cannot postpone.

Antenatal Care

Check-ups through every month of pregnancy — weight, blood pressure, growth and worries. Unhurried time, in a room where questions are welcome.

Deliveries

Normal deliveries and caesarean sections, attended by our own team. Calm hands, a clean room, and the family kept informed throughout.

Gynaecology

Women's health beyond pregnancy — examinations, treatment and gynaecological surgery, with the privacy every woman deserves.

General Surgery

The operations a family cannot keep putting off — assessed honestly, explained plainly, and done in our own operation theatre.

Admissions & In-Patient Care

Beds for mothers and post-operative patients, with attendants allowed to stay close. Nursing that checks on you without being asked.

Post-Natal Follow-Up

Care does not stop at discharge. Mother and baby checks after you go home, and a number you can call when something worries you.

Our Promise

Why families choose us.

Honest advice, always.

If a normal delivery is possible, we will not push you towards an operation. If a case needs a bigger centre, we say so early and send your records with you.

Charges explained first.

Clean, calm, dignified.

A hospital should lower your pulse, not raise it. Clean rooms, privacy for every mother, and staff who speak to families with respect.

Here for the long run.

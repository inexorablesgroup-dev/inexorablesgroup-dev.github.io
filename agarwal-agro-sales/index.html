On Station Road Since 1986

Four Decades.
Same Counter.

A farm machine is not bought twice. That is why Agarwal Agro Sales (Gobind) has never sold a machine it would not stand behind — and why the sons of our first customers still walk through the same door.

We opened opposite Lohia Dharamshala in 1986, when most of these machines were new to Gonda's fields. Since then the seasons have changed, the models have changed, and the one thing that hasn't is who answers when something needs fixing.

Buy here once. Come back for the next one. That has been the whole business plan.

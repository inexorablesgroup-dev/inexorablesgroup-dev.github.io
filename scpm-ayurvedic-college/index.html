The College
A physician is not made by lectures alone. Why the hospital sits on the campus

Ayurveda is learned at the bedside.

SCPM Ayurvedic Medical College & Hospital is the district of Gonda’s ayurvedic medical college: a Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery read over five and a half years, the last of which is a compulsory rotatory internship. The sanctioned intake is one hundred students to a batch. What distinguishes the place is not the syllabus, which every recognised college shares, but the ward at the end of the corridor.

The attached ayurvedic hospital runs a hundred beds with outpatient and inpatient services, a pharmacy, diagnostics and a twenty-four-hour ambulance. Students read the Samhitas in the morning and see patients in the afternoon, on the same grounds, under supervision. The classical texts are taught in full — Samhita, Sharir, Dravyaguna — before any shortcut is permitted, and the medicinal plants of the Dravyaguna syllabus grow in a herbal garden a short walk from the lecture hall, because a plant must be seen to be understood.

The college is approved by the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine and affiliated to Mahayogi Guru Gorakhnath AYUSH University, Gorakhpur. Admission is through NEET-UG followed by the UP AYUSH counselling conducted by the Uttar Pradesh State Ayush Society. It is one arm of the SCPM Group of Hospital & Education, which runs nursing, paramedical and pharmacy institutes from the same Lucknow Road campus.

The Programmes

One degree, read across nine departments.

The B.A.M.S. is taught through the departments prescribed by the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine. Each is listed below as it appears on the course.

Ten entries

01 B.A.M.S. — the degree Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery, five and a half years including a one-year compulsory rotatory internship. Sanctioned intake one hundred seats. 02 Samhita & Siddhanta The classical texts and the first principles of ayurveda, taught in full before clinical work begins. 03 Rachana Sharir Anatomy, studied in the dissection hall and the specimen museums alongside the prescribed texts. 04 Dravyaguna Materia medica and the identification of medicinal plants, taught partly in the college’s own herbal garden. 05 Kayachikitsa General medicine — the largest share of hospital hours, seen across the outpatient and inpatient departments. 06 Shalya Tantra Surgery in the ayurvedic tradition, taught with the parasurgical procedures the syllabus prescribes. 07 Prasuti Tantra & Stri Roga Obstetrics, gynaecology and the care of women, taught on the wards of the attached hospital. 08 Kaumarbhritya Paediatrics and the ayurvedic care of children, from infancy through adolescence. 09 Agada Tantra Toxicology and medical jurisprudence, including the forensic subjects the degree requires. 10 Swasthavritta & Yoga Preventive medicine and public health, practised daily on the ground rather than read once before an examination.

Teaching & Mentorship “
A student who has never seen the illness has only read about the cure.

Teaching here is organised into the departments the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine prescribes, and each of them owns a share of the hospital week. That is the whole argument for putting a hundred beds beside a lecture hall: the diagnosis a student watches being made in the outpatient department on Tuesday is the chapter they read on Monday night.

Laboratories, museums, the dissection hall and the herbal garden carry the practical half of the syllabus. Faculty appointments follow the norms set for a recognised ayurvedic college, and the current teaching roster, along with the year’s permission letter, may be seen at the office by any candidate or parent who asks for it.

Admissions

Four steps, in order.

Admission to the B.A.M.S. programme runs through the state counselling process. Nothing here can be short-circuited, but the waiting can be made a good deal less anxious.

1

Qualify NEET-UG

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test is the single entrance route to a B.A.M.S. seat. Keep the scorecard and the registration details to hand.

2

Register for UP AYUSH counselling

Counselling is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh State Ayush Society. Registration, choice-filling and the security deposit are completed on the counselling portal within the published window.

3

Seat allotment and reporting

Seats are allotted round by round against the choices filled. An allotment carries a reporting date, and a missed reporting date usually costs the seat.

4

Verification at the college

Report at Lucknow Road, Haripur with the original documents for verification and fee submission. The admissions desk will confirm the current list before you travel.

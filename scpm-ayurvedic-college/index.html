Campus & Hospital

Everything a BAMS degree requires.

Lucknow Road, Haripur — college, hospital, hostels and herbal garden on one campus.

B.A.M.S. — 5.5 Years

The full Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery, including a one-year compulsory rotatory internship. Intake: 100 seats.

100-Bed Ayurvedic Hospital

OPD and IPD running on campus, with pharmacy, diagnostics and a 24-hour ambulance. Students learn on real patients, not case sheets.

Herbal Garden & Museums

A living Dravyaguna garden of medicinal plants, plus museums for specimens and instruments. Identification learned by hand, not from a page.

Library, Labs & Dissection Hall

Classical Samhitas and modern references on the same shelves, with laboratories and a Rachana Sharir dissection hall alongside.

Swasthavritta & Yoga

Preventive medicine taught the way it is meant to be — daily practice on the ground, not a chapter read once before an examination.

Hostels, Mess & Transport

Separate boys’ and girls’ hostels on campus, with mess, security, auditorium and college buses. Outstation students settle in from day one.

The Signature Rhythm

A day at SCPM.

Text in the morning, patients in the afternoon. This is how a BAMS student’s day is shaped here.

EARLY MORNING

Yoga and the herbal garden

Swasthavritta begins the day — practice on the ground, then a walk through the medicinal garden while the leaves are still wet.

MORNING

Samhita and Siddhanta

Classical lectures when minds are freshest — Charaka, Sushruta, the principles that everything after this depends on.

FORENOON

Sharir, labs and dissection hall

Rachana and Kriya Sharir taught where they belong — over specimens, slides and the dissection table.

MIDDAY

Mess break on campus

Hostellers and day scholars at the same tables. Half the learning in a medical college happens over a plate.

AFTERNOON

Hospital rounds — OPD and IPD

The part no textbook replaces. Kayachikitsa, Shalya, Prasuti and Kaumarbhritya cases seen under supervision, on campus.

EVENING

Library, hostel — and a message home

Reading hours in the library, then back to the hostel. Attendance, notices and fee reminders reach parents on WhatsApp the same evening.

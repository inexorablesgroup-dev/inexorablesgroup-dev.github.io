The Salon

A National Standard, On Your Street.

For years, a proper salon appointment in Gonda meant settling for less — or a day trip to a bigger city. Lakme Salon Gonda ends that.

We are the Gonda outlet of Lakme Salon, India's largest salon chain. That means the same service menu, the same professional products and the same trained-stylist standards you would find in a metro — on the first floor of Pratap Complex at Guddumal Chauraha.

A ladies-only space, built to be calm and private. Skin, hair, makeup and bridal, all under one roof.

What You Can Expect

  • A ladies-only salon — private, comfortable, unhurried.
  • Lakme brand products and service standards throughout.
  • A consultation before every service — never a surprise result.
  • Book by phone or WhatsApp — walk-ins welcome when we have a chair.

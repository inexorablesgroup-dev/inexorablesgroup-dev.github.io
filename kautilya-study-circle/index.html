The method

Teach the syllabus. Then teach the paper.

A government job is won on syllabus, not luck. Everything here is built around one idea: cover the paper completely, then rehearse it until speed stops being the problem.

Paper-by-paper syllabus coverage

Each track is planned against its own notification — the sections that carry marks get the hours, not the ones that are easy to teach.

Batches sized for doubts

A doubt raised today is answered today. Faculty stay back for the questions a student would not ask in front of a full class.

Two shifts, one syllabus

Morning and evening batches run the same plan with the same faculty, so college or a job is never the reason a student drops out.

Practice on the real pattern

Regular mock papers written to the exam's own format, then discussed question by question. The exam rewards speed as much as knowledge.

How It Works

Three Steps. One Registry.

The same process whether you are buying your first plot or selling family land.

01 — TELL US

Budget and Locality.

One conversation: what you want the land for, which side of Gonda suits you, and what you are comfortable spending. We tell you straight away what that budget really buys.

02 — SEE IT

We Walk the Land.

We shortlist only what actually fits and take you there — boundary, approach road, water, neighbours, and what the papers say. No pressure to decide on the spot.

03 — CLOSE IT

Through to Registry.

Rate settled, bainama drawn, stamp duty and registry handled, mutation followed up after. You are not left alone at the tehsil.

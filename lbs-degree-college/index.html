Six decades in the service of Gonda

The college opened on 2 October 1966, founded in the memory of Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri and started by the then District Magistrate, Shri Rajendra Nath. It has taught in Gonda city ever since — a public, co-educational, government-aided institution where the medium of instruction runs in both Hindi and English, and where the first graduate in a family is still a common sight at the counter.

Teaching today is spread across three campuses in the city — the Main Campus at Civil Lines, the Science Campus and the B.Ed Campus. Six faculties operate between them: Arts & Humanities, Science, Commerce & Management, Education, Engineering & Technology and Agriculture. Fourteen departments sit within Arts alone, among them English, History and Psychology.

Degrees run from B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.B.A., B.C.A. and B.Sc. (Agriculture) through B.Ed., and on to the postgraduate M.A. and M.Com. programmes that give the college its second name in the district. The college is affiliated to Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur, is UGC recognised and holds NAAC accreditation, and maintains an IQAC cell, alumni registration and public RTI disclosures.

Teaching
A college is measured less by its buildings than by the hours a teacher gives after the lecture has ended.

Departments here are small enough that a student is known by name, and old enough that the same subject has been taught in the same rooms for decades. Six faculties, fourteen departments in Arts alone, and a teaching staff spread across three campuses — the college has never published a headcount, and this demonstration does not invent one.

What can be said plainly is how the work is organised: an IQAC cell that carries the college’s quality documentation, departmental email addresses for every faculty, a dedicated admission enquiry line, and a public record of RTI disclosures and tender notices. The names of individual teachers are deliberately left out of this concept — the college publishes them itself, faculty by faculty.

Two languages

Teaching runs in Hindi and English, which is why a student from a village school and a student from a city school sit in the same lecture.

Three campuses

Arts and Commerce at the Main Campus, the sciences on the Science Campus, and teacher training on the B.Ed Campus.

One university

Syllabus, examinations and degrees follow Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur, under UGC recognition.

Enquiries

Questions students ask

Which courses can I take admission in?
Six faculties: Arts & Humanities, Science, Commerce & Management, Education, Engineering & Technology (Computer Applications) and Agriculture. Degrees include B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.B.A., B.C.A., B.Sc. (Agriculture) and B.Ed., with postgraduate M.A. and M.Com. programmes.
How does admission work?
Register online through the college admission portal or at the Civil Lines office, then bring your marksheets and documents for verification, and complete the fee formalities. Merit and university norms decide the final list — ask at the counter for the current session’s dates.
Which university awards the degree?
The college is affiliated to Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur, and is UGC recognised with NAAC accreditation. Examinations and degrees follow the university’s schedule.
How will I hear about exam forms and results?
Notices are put up on the campus boards and on the college website. This concept demonstration shows how the same notices could reach you course-wise on WhatsApp — exam-form deadlines, fee dates, scholarship windows and results.
Where is the college, and which campus do I go to?
The Main Campus is at Civil Lines, Gonda 271003. The Science and B.Ed campuses are separate — ask at the Main Campus counter which one your course is taught on, or call 05262-232994.
Is teaching in Hindi or in English?
Both. The college is government-aided and co-educational, and teaches in Hindi and English across its faculties.

Our Story

Six decades in the service of Gonda.

Opened on 2 October 1966, in the memory of Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri.

It began with Arts alone. Today it teaches six faculties across three campuses.

Government-aided, co-educational, taught in Hindi and English — so no one is priced or worded out.

NAAC accredited, affiliated to Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur.

For most families in Gonda, a degree still begins here.

Study at LBS

Six faculties. One college.

From B.A. to M.Com., from a chemistry bench to an agriculture field — the whole degree journey, in Gonda city.

Arts & Humanities

Fourteen departments — Hindi, English, History, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, Economics and more. B.A. through M.A.

Faculty of Science

Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology and Mathematics. B.Sc. and M.Sc. programmes with working teaching laboratories.

Commerce & Management

B.Com., M.Com. and B.B.A. — accountancy, business studies and management for students who want a trade, not just a certificate.

Engineering & Technology

Computer Applications, including B.C.A. — the practical, employable side of the college, taught on machines.

Faculty of Education

A dedicated B.Ed. campus, training the next generation of teachers for schools across the district.

Faculty of Agriculture

B.Sc. (Agriculture) — a degree that speaks directly to the district it sits in, for students from farming families.

10:30 AM

Laboratories and computer rooms

Physics, chemistry and botany practicals on the Science Campus; B.C.A. students at the machines.

12:00 PM

1:30 PM

Notices go out — on WhatsApp

Exam dates, scholarship windows and result announcements reaching students where they actually look.

Questions Students Ask

Answers, straight.

Six faculties: Arts & Humanities, Science, Commerce & Management, Education, Engineering & Technology (Computer Applications) and Agriculture. Degrees include B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.B.A., B.C.A., B.Sc. (Agriculture) and B.Ed., with postgraduate M.A. and M.Com. programmes.

Register online through the college admission portal or at the Civil Lines office, then bring your marksheets and documents for verification, and complete the fee formalities. Merit and university norms decide the final list — ask at the counter for the current session’s dates.

The college is affiliated to Maa Pateshwari University, Balrampur, and is UGC recognised with NAAC accreditation. Examinations and degrees follow the university’s schedule.

Notices are put up on the campus boards and on the college website. This concept demo shows how the same notices could reach you course-wise on WhatsApp — exam-form deadlines, fee dates, scholarship windows and results.

The Main Campus is at Civil Lines, Gonda 271003. The Science and B.Ed campuses are separate — ask at the Main Campus counter which one your course is taught on, or call 05262-232994.

The Shop

A Name the Chowk Already Knows.

Some shops just stack boxes. Agarwal Footwear chooses them. Every pair on our racks is picked for the thing that actually matters after a week of wearing it — the sole, the stitch, the shape it holds.

A child's first school shoe. A pair of sandals for the festival. A carton of socks for the shop down the road. We serve the family at the counter and the trader at the back door with the same straight rate.

Try the fit before you pay. Take your time. Nobody here rushes a customer.

Kids' Footwear

Socks & Hosiery

Everyday Chappals

Polish & Shoe Care

Polishes, creams and brushes. A good pair lasts twice as long when it's looked after — so we keep the things that look after it.

Wholesale Supply

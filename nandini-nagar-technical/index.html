Admissions

Start with one message.

No agents, no guesswork. Four steps between a message and your first lecture.

01 /

Send the enquiry

Name, programme, phone. The admissions desk replies with eligibility, the fee schedule and hostel details.

02 /

Check eligibility

B.Tech needs 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Maths plus a JEE Main score. MBA needs a bachelor’s degree and an entrance score. The office confirms the diploma route.

03 /

Walk the campus

Come to Nawabganj on a working day and see the labs, the hostel and the workshops before you commit. Bring a parent.

04 /

Counselling & documents

Seats are allotted through state counselling against your score. Then mark sheets, transfer certificate, category certificate — and the semester begins.

Questions Students Ask

Answers, straight.

A four-year B.Tech in five branches — Civil, Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics and Mechanical — a two-year MBA, and a three-year polytechnic diploma. All run under affiliation to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow.

B.Tech and MBA seats are filled through state counselling against your entrance score — JEE Main for B.Tech, and CAT, MAT, XAT or CMAT for MBA. Diploma admission is handled directly at the campus office. Send an enquiry first and the desk will tell you exactly which route applies to you.

Yes — the campus has hostel accommodation along with a canteen, a medical facility, an auditorium and sports grounds. Ask the office for current room availability and charges when you visit.

Fees differ by programme and are payable in instalments. Message the admissions desk with the course you want and you will get the current fee schedule, including hostel charges, in writing.

At Nawabganj in Gonda district, Uttar Pradesh 271303 — roughly 25 km from Gonda city, on the shared campus of the Nandini Nagar group of institutions. Use Get Directions below and the map will take you to the gate.

Lateral entry into the second year of B.Tech is a standard route for polytechnic diploma holders under AKTU. The admissions office will confirm the current process and the counselling route that applies in your session.

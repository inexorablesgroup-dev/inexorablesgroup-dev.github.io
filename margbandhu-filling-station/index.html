The Pump

Company fuel, straight from the depot gate.

Margbandhu Filling Station stands on Bahraich Road at Jankinagar, right beside the HP petroleum depot — an authorised Hindustan Petroleum retail outlet serving Gonda's commuters, the Bahraich highway, and the commercial traffic that moves through this stretch every day.

The nozzle reads zero before it reads anything else. Meters sealed and checked to HPCL standard, receipts on request, digital payment accepted at the pump. Nothing about how you are served here should ever need explaining.

Open twenty-four hours. Late shift, early load, or a tank that ran dry at two in the morning — the lights are on.

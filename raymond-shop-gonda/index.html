The Store

Gonda's Address for a Proper Suit.

The Raymond Shop format has dressed India since 1958, and Bharatpuri is where it lives in Gonda — 880 Station Road, near the District Hospital. Suiting and shirting by the metre, ready-to-wear formals off the rack, and a tailoring service that turns the two into one garment made for you.

Customers here talk about the same two things again and again: the fabric range, and the readymade formal collection. That is the whole business in one line — good cloth, and clothes that fit the occasion.

Open every day, 10:30 am to 9:30 pm. Bring the wedding card, bring the family, take your time.

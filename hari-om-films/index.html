The Studio

A Wedding Passes. The Film Doesn't.

The day itself goes by in a blur — the haldi, the baraat, your mother's face at the vidaai. Our job at Hari Om Films is to make sure you actually get to see it.

We shoot candid, not posed-and-frozen. And because our own mixing lab sits right here at Purani Sabji Mandi, your footage never leaves our hands — we shoot it, we cut it, we hand it to you.

Studio work, portraits and general videography run alongside, through our Hari Om Photo Studio And Videography unit at the same address.

What You Can Count On

  • Your date is blocked the moment you book — never double-booked.
  • Package agreed in writing before the shoot. No surprise charges.
  • Shot and edited in-house — your footage never goes to an outside lab.
  • Your photos and films stay yours — shared only with your permission.

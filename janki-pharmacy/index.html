What We Handle

Specialised lines, held locally

Anti-cancer stock is where most district distribution quietly stops. It is high in value, exacting in handling, and slow to move — so it gets left to the metros, and a Gonda family ends up travelling for a single strip.

Carrying it is a decision about the district, not about margin. It sits alongside veterinary and homeopathic lines that retailers otherwise chase across three suppliers.

Equipment on the same order

Clinics and nursing homes rarely need only medicine. Consumables, instruments and equipment go on the same line as the pharmaceutical order — one supplier, one conversation, one delivery to plan around.

Who we are

We believe a school is judged by what it publishes, not what it promises.

So here is ours: a CBSE campus opened in 2018, carrying the standard of the Sunbeam Group of Varanasi — and results the school prints openly, batch after batch.

The Sunbeam Group began in Varanasi in 1972. Gonda got its own campus in 2018, managed locally by Scholars Educational Trust. CBSE affiliation followed — High School in 2022, Intermediate in 2023.

Board batches here are still small and closely taught, with a district topper in Humanities among them. Science, Commerce and Humanities all run in the senior wing, so a child never has to leave to find their stream.

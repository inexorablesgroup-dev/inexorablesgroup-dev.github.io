The Signature Rhythm

A day at Sunbeam Gonda.

Structure is a kindness. Here is the shape of your child’s day on our campus.

First Bell

Assembly on the grounds

Prayer, the day’s news read out by students, and a quiet minute before class begins. Timings differ for the junior wing.

Morning

Core hours — when minds are freshest

Mathematics, science and languages take the morning in air-conditioned classrooms with interactive boards.

Mid-day

Lab & library block

Beakers in Chemistry, keyboards in the Computer lab, silence in the library. The loudest and quietest hours, side by side.

Break

Lunch and open air

Tiffins open, friendships form. Teachers sit with their classes — the best conversations happen here.

Afternoon

Sport, taekwondo & arts

Cricket, basketball, the taekwondo mat, music and sketchbooks. Every child picks a lane and owns it for the year.

Dispersal

Buses out — and a message home

GPS-fitted buses leave on route, on time. Parents get a WhatsApp update before the child reaches the gate.

Questions Parents Ask

Answers, straight.

Pre-Nursery through Class XII, subject to seats in each section. The senior wing offers Science, Commerce and Humanities. Mid-session transfers are considered case by case — bring the previous school’s report card and transfer certificate when you visit.

Three steps: send an enquiry or visit the campus office, a short interaction with the child (senior classes take a written assessment), then document verification and fee submission. Call +91 73111 73001 for the current session’s dates and prospectus.

Our buses run on all major routes across Gonda and are GPS-fitted. Share your address at the office and we will confirm the nearest pickup point and timing.

Parent-teacher meetings through the year, a diary that actually gets checked, and WhatsApp updates for results, notices and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from us first.

Air-conditioned classrooms with interactive boards, six laboratories — Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Composite Science and Computer — a full library, sports grounds and CCTV throughout. School timings differ by wing; call the office for the current schedule.

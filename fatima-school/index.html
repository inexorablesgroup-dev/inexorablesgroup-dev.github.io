The Fatima Rhythm

Life at Fatima.

Structure is a kindness. Here is the shape of a day your child will come to know by heart.

Morning

Assembly, prayer and the first bell

The school gathers, the day is read out, and a minute of stillness settles a courtyard full of restless minds before the first class.

Core Hours

Mathematics, science and languages

The freshest hours go to the hardest subjects. No child is moved on until the concept has actually landed.

Lab Block

Beakers, keyboards, smart boards

Physics, biology and chemistry are performed, not recited. The computer lab and the smart classrooms run on a campus wired end to end.

Midday

Cafeteria and the open ground

Tiffins open, friendships form. The cafeteria and the grounds do as much work as any classroom.

Afternoon

Sport, art, music and dance

Games on the field, sketchbooks in the art room, the audio-visual hall in use. Every child picks a lane and keeps it for the year.

Dispersal

Vans out — and a message home

The school van fleet rolls out to its pick-up points, attendant on board. Parents get a WhatsApp update before the child reaches the gate.

Inside the gates.

The library, the laboratories, the grounds — and the last morning in uniform, when a Fatima education finally pays out.

Questions Parents Ask

Answers, straight.

We admit from LKG through Class XII under the CBSE curriculum, subject to seats in each section. LKG intake runs on a published schedule each year; higher classes are considered case by case — bring the previous school’s report card when you visit.

Send an enquiry or visit the school office, collect and complete the registration form, and then attend on the day allotted to your child’s section to finish the formalities. The office shares the current document checklist and the dates for each section when you register — the allotted day matters, so do come on it.

The school runs its own van fleet across Gonda town, with an attendant on board every vehicle. Share your address at the office and the transport in-charge will confirm the nearest pick-up point and its timing. Vans do not wait, so children should reach the point a few minutes early.

Parent-teacher meetings, a diary that actually gets checked, an online parent portal, and WhatsApp updates for results, circulars and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from us first.

The school office receives parents on working days between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM — that is the window used for registration, document submission and admission enquiries. You can also reach us on 9151324447, by call or on WhatsApp.

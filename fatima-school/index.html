Our Story

Fifty years, one address, one standard.

Fatima Senior Secondary School has stood on Circular Road, Bargaon, since 1976. It is a co-educational, English-medium school affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education under affiliation number 2130053, and it is run by the Catholic Diocese of Lucknow — an institution, not a proprietorship.

Children join in the junior classes and stay through to the board examinations of Class XII. Mind, body and spirit are held together here, never one at the cost of the others; discipline comes from respect rather than fear, and results follow because the fundamentals were never rushed.

I feel proud to be a part of this esteemed institution which has the history of forty-eight years.
Rev. Fr. Arun Moras · Principal

Joining Us

How admission works.

Registration for the junior classes runs on a published schedule each year; the higher classes are considered as seats allow.

I.

Send an enquiry

Tell the office the child’s name, the class you are applying for and a number that reaches you. It takes under a minute, and it can be done at any hour.

II.

Collect the registration form

The school office issues and receives registration forms on working days, between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM, at the Circular Road campus.

III.

Attend on your allotted day

Each section is given its own day for the admission formalities. The office confirms yours when you register — the allotted day matters, so do come on it.

IV.

Complete the formalities

Bring the documents on the checklist the office shares, including the previous school’s report card where one applies, and the seat is confirmed for the session.

Questions Parents Ask

Answers, plainly given.

We admit from LKG through Class XII under the CBSE curriculum, subject to seats in each section. The junior intake runs on a published schedule each year; higher classes are considered case by case — bring the previous school’s report card when you visit.

Send an enquiry or visit the school office, collect and complete the registration form, and then attend on the day allotted to your child’s section to finish the formalities. The office shares the current document checklist and the dates for each section when you register — the allotted day matters, so do come on it.

The school runs its own fleet of four vans across Gonda town, with an attendant on board every vehicle. Share your address at the office and the transport in-charge will confirm the nearest pick-up point and its timing. The vans do not wait, so children should reach the point a few minutes early.

Parent-teacher meetings, a diary that actually gets checked, an online parent portal, and WhatsApp updates for results, circulars and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from the school first.

The school office receives parents on working days between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM — the window used for registration, document submission and admission enquiries. You can also reach the office on 9151324447, by call or on WhatsApp.

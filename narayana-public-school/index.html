Academics

Structure is a kindness.
Here is the shape of a day.

01 8:00 AM

Gates open, assembly begins

Prayer, notices and a settled start — the day begins together before the first bell.

02 Morning

Core hours, when minds are freshest

Mathematics, science and languages take the morning. No child is moved on until the concept lands.

03 Late morning

Laboratory periods

Hands on beakers and keyboards. Science and computer periods where the apparatus actually comes out of the cupboard.

04 Midday

Break, and the conversations in it

Tiffins open and friendships form. Supervised, unhurried, and often where the best conversations of the day happen.

05 Afternoon

Art, activity & assignments

Drawing, activity periods, and the day’s work posted to Google Classroom so nothing depends on a torn-out notebook page.

06 3:00 PM

Dispersal — and a message home

The last bell at three. With WhatsApp updates switched on, a notice or reminder can reach parents before the child reaches the gate.

Questions & directions

Ask early,
while there is still time to visit.

Which board does the school follow?

CBSE, New Delhi. Our affiliation number is 2131758 and our school code is 70484 — you can verify both on the CBSE website before you enquire. The affiliation is at senior secondary level, so a child can study here right through to the Class XII boards.

How does the admission process work?

Send an enquiry here or walk into the school office during working hours, and the staff will take you through the current steps, the class-wise position and the fee structure. Carry the previous school’s report card and transfer certificate if your child is joining mid-way.

Where exactly is the school?

Near Avas Vikas Colony, Phase III, Gonda — 271002. Use the Get directions button and your phone will take you to the gate. For transport from your locality, ask at the office and share your address; routes are confirmed case by case.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Parent-teacher meetings, a diary that actually gets checked, and coursework visible on Google Classroom — with WhatsApp updates for results, notices and fee reminders as the next step. If something needs your attention, you should hear it from the school first — not from another parent.

What are the school timings?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Saturday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Closed on Sunday. The office keeps the same hours — for a visit, late morning is the calmest time to come.

A word from the school

Founded by a teacher,
for teaching.

The school was founded in 1970 by the late Mr. J.K. Nelson, formerly principal of Thompson Inter College, beside the Dukh Haran Nath temple in Patel Nagar. It became the oldest convent school of the city — and stayed a self-financed institution, answerable only to the families it serves.

Today Ravi Children’s Academy is a CBSE senior secondary school across two campuses, carrying a child from the primary wing all the way to the board. Academics first — but never academics alone. All-round development, personality, and the confidence to speak up in any room.

More than half a century of Gonda families have walked through these gates. The standard has not been allowed to move.

Life at the Academy

Everything a childhood
deserves.

Classrooms, laboratories, the library and the buses — what the school actually runs, held to the same standard on both campuses.

Academics

An English-medium CBSE curriculum taught patiently and thoroughly, right through to Class XII. Concepts first, marks after — in that order, always.

Care & Attention

Teachers who have known the child since the primary wing track more than marks — attendance, conduct and growth. If something needs a parent’s attention, it comes from the school first.

Laboratories

Science and computer laboratories on campus. Children learn science by doing it — the experiment is performed, not read aloud from a textbook.

Sports & Activities

Games, the field and the stage. Speaking and bearing are treated as subjects here, not as lucky accidents of temperament.

Library & Transport

A quiet reading room with full shelves, and school buses running the routes across Gonda town. Tell the office your locality and the nearest pickup point is confirmed.

Values

The motto is not decoration. Excellence made a habit means the small standards — punctuality, honesty, turnout — are kept every single day.

Admissions

Begin with
one enquiry.

Tell the office about your child. Four steps, no queue, and nothing that cannot be started from a phone at nine in the evening.

1

Enquire

Send the form below, or call the office. Give the child’s name and the class you are looking at — nothing more is needed yet.

3

Documents

The previous school’s report card, transfer certificate, birth record and photographs, verified at the office counter.

Confirmation

Fee submission, and the seat is held. Most admissions complete within a week of the first visit.

Questions parents ask

Answers,
straight.

Admissions are taken across the primary wing and right through to Class XII, subject to seats in each section. Mid-session transfers are considered case by case — bring the previous school’s report card when you visit.

Send an enquiry or visit the office, then a short interaction with the child (senior classes take a written assessment), then document verification and fee submission. Most admissions complete within a week.

School buses cover the main routes across Gonda town and nearby localities. Share your address at the office and the nearest pickup point and timing will be confirmed.

Regular parent-teacher meetings, a diary that actually gets checked, and — in the version this demo proposes — WhatsApp updates for results, notices and fee reminders. If something needs your attention, you hear it from the school first.

The school day runs from 7:50 AM to 1:50 PM, with the break from 10:50 to 11:05 AM. The administrative office stays open from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM for visits, admissions and enquiries.

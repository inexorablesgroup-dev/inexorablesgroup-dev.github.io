Gonda's Own Lenskart Store.

Gonda has never been short of opticians. What it did not have was a national eyewear chain. Now it does. Lenskart - Civil Lines Road Gonda sits in the middle of the city — opposite Shaheed-e-Azam Bhagat Singh Inter College, on the road you already pass every day.

Frames, lenses and warranty all come through the Lenskart chain — the same supply the brand runs everywhere in India. The difference here is that you can walk in, try the frames on your own face, and leave wearing the right one.

Open eleven hours a day, seven days a week. Come after work. We will still be here.

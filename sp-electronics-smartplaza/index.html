Questions? Answers.

EMI is available on most televisions, appliances and phones through card and consumer-finance options. Bring your ID and we will check your eligibility at the counter within minutes — or send us the model on WhatsApp first and we will reply with the monthly figure.

Yes. We are Gonda's Samsung SmartPlaza — Samsung's own brand-exclusive showroom format, which the company licenses to one dealer per town. Everything on our floor is authorised stock, billed with GST and covered by the full Samsung India warranty.

Not for cooling. Our showroom is Samsung for televisions, refrigerators, phones and appliances — but on air conditioners we are a multi-brand dealer, stocking Daikin, Mitsubishi and Voltas. That means you get an honest comparison of tonnage, star rating and price instead of one brand's answer.

Call us first, not the helpline. Every product carries its full manufacturer warranty, and we raise and follow up the service request with the brand on your behalf. Your bill from this counter is all the proof you need.

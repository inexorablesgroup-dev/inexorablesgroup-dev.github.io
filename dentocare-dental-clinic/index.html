Our Story

Dental care that doesn't make you travel for it.

A cracked tooth used to mean a bus to Lucknow.

One trip for the X-ray. Another for the treatment. A third to collect the crown.

Dentocare was built to end that journey.

Root canal specialists, implantologists, orthodontists, crown-and-denture work, children's dentistry, gum treatment and oral surgery — every stream, in one clinic, in Gonda.

Treatments

One roof. Every speciality.

From a child's first filling to a full set of implants — walk in once, and let us take it from there.

Root Canal Treatment

Deep decay and abscessed teeth treated by a root canal specialist — the tooth saved rather than pulled, and the pain gone the same visit.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns fitted by our crown-and-bridge specialist — with same-day delivery on crown work, so a broken tooth doesn't cost you a second trip.

Dental Implants

A missing tooth replaced at the root, not just covered over. Planned and placed in-house by our implantologist, and built to chew like the real thing.

Braces & Orthodontics

Crowded, gapped or protruding teeth straightened under an orthodontist's plan — with the whole course reviewed here in Gonda, month after month.

Dentures & White Fillings

Full and partial dentures fitted for comfort, and tooth-coloured white fillings that repair a cavity without announcing it to the room.

Kids, Gums & Oral Screening

Children's dentistry with a light touch, gum treatment before teeth loosen, and oral cancer screening — the check that catches what nothing else will.

Our Promise

Why patients come back.

Save the tooth first.

Extraction is the last option here, not the quick one. If a tooth can be treated and kept, we will tell you how — and if it truly cannot, we'll say that plainly too.

Costs before the chair.

What the treatment involves, how many sittings it takes and what it will cost — explained before anything begins, not discovered at the counter.

Pain handled gently.

Most people put off a dentist because of one bad memory. Numbing done properly, unhurried hands, and a clinic that lowers your pulse instead of raising it.

The whole mouth, for years.

Your file, your X-rays, your history — kept. Crowns, braces and implants are long relationships, and yours stays with one clinic in your own town.

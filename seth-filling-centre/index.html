The Forecourt

A pump the city already trusts.

Seth Filling Centre is an authorised IndianOil retail outlet on the Lucknow–Colonelganj–Gonda road, in Civil Lines — the stretch every commuter, transporter and government vehicle in Gonda crosses.

Zero shown before every fill. Full measure, every time. The reading starts at zero, you watch it run, and the slip matches the pump. That is the whole promise, and it is the reason regulars keep coming back to this forecourt instead of the next one.

Beyond the nozzle: lubricants, air and water, digital payment, and fuel supplied on account to fleets and departments across the district.

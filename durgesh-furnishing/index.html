Our Story

A Gonda shop that became a brand.

Most mattress shops sell what a company sends them. We make ours. We Care You is our own brand, built at Golaganj and carried today by a dealer network across eastern Uttar Pradesh.

That means the person who sells you the mattress knows what is inside it — the coir, the bonded foam, the memory foam, the spring unit. And it means we can cut one to your bed instead of asking your bed to fit the mattress.

Back pain is the reason most people walk in. A proper night's sleep is the reason they come back for the second one.

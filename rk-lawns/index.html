Our Story

One Gate.
The Whole Function.

The baraat at the gate. The garland under the lights. The family that has to sleep somewhere afterwards. RK Lawns was built so all three happen at one address.

An air-conditioned banquet hall for the reception, an open lawn for the wedding itself, and rooms upstairs for the guests who have come from out of town — no convoy across Gonda at midnight.

Set on Balrampur Road near the Shubhagpur crossing, minutes from the railway station. Easy for the city to find, easier still for the guests arriving by train.

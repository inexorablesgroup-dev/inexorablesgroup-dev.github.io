The method

Concepts first. Speed after.

A student sitting in Gonda writes the same paper as a student sitting in Kota. Everything below exists because that is treated as obvious here.

Derivation before shortcut

Nobody moves on until the derivation lands. Tricks are taught after the reasoning, never instead of it.

A test every week

Weekly tests on the week’s syllabus, then a session where the wrong answers get taken apart properly.

Doubt hours, every day

The questions a student will not ask in a full room get asked one to one, at the teacher’s desk.

Notes and results in one login

The student portal carries schedules, material and test results, so a missed class is not a missed chapter.

Questions parents ask

Answers, straight.

Foundation for Class 10 is ₹18,000 for one year. The Class 12 programme is ₹30,000 for one year. The two-year NEET & JEE Foundation course is ₹50,000. Fees are published openly — ask at the office about instalment options.

Send an enquiry or walk in to the Circular Road office. We talk through the student’s current level and which batch fits, then complete the form and fee. Ask about sitting in on a class before you decide.

A faculty of seven, each named with their subject — engineers for Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, a BAMS doctor for Biology, and dedicated Science and English teachers. The founder, Dr. Shubham Pandey, teaches Biology himself. You can meet the subject teacher before you enrol.

Weekly tests, a student portal with schedules and results, and WhatsApp updates for scores, attendance and fee due-dates. If a student starts slipping, you hear it before the exam, not after.

The institute runs 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, in morning and evening batches so school-going students can attend either side of their school day. Ask at the office which slot has seats for your course.

The two-year programme covers the shared Class 11–12 foundation across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology, with subject teachers for each. Which papers a student targets is settled with the faculty at the time of admission.

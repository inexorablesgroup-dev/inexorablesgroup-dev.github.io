The Signature Rhythm

A day at Torque.

Doors open at 7 in the morning and close at 8 at night. Here is what fills the hours in between.

7:00 AM

Doors open

The early batch is already seated. School-going students take their session before the school bell, not after it.

8:30 AM

Concept hours

Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics taught from first principles. Nobody moves on until the derivation lands.

11:30 AM

Problem practice

Sheets, past papers and timed sets. The gap between understanding a topic and scoring on it closes here.

1:00 PM

Break — and the doubt queue

The questions students will not ask in a full room get asked here, one to one, at the teacher’s desk.

3:00 PM

The second shift

School is over, the afternoon and evening batches start. Foundation and Class 12 groups run through to the last session of the day.

8:00 PM

Close — and a message home

Last batch ends. Parents get a WhatsApp update on attendance and the week’s test result, without having to ask.

Our Work

It Starts With Flowers.

A wedding is remembered by how it looked. The gate the baraat walks through. The mandap. The stage where everyone takes their photos. That is the work we do, and flower art is where Decorator Rafeeq began.

From there it grew into full decoration and planning — tent and pandal, lighting, seating, stage, car and gate. One team handles the whole setup, so the family isn't chasing four different people on the morning of the function.

How It Works

Three Steps. Zero Panic.

The same process for one haldi or a four-day wedding.

01 — DATE & VENUE

Tell Us The Date.

Send the date, the function and where it is being held. We confirm availability, then look at the venue — a lawn, a hall and a home each need a different plan.

02 — DESIGN & RATE

We Show You The Setup.

Flowers, colours, stage and lighting decided together, with the rate written down before anything begins. What you approve is what you get.

03 — SETUP & CLEAR

We Finish Early.

Our team reaches ahead of time, sets everything up, stays through the function, and clears it afterwards. You only attend the wedding.

The Company

A Home On Wheels.

The night journey out of Gonda used to mean a hard seat and a long, sleepless road. Vaishali Expresso Pvt Ltd (Vaishali Express) was built to end that.

Our AC sleeper coaches run daily from the Circular Road counter in Gonda to Delhi and Lucknow, with a counter network reaching Bahraich, Balrampur, Tulsipur, Colonelganj, Basti, Gorakhpur and Azamgarh. Every coach is GPS tracked and stocked with WiFi, blankets, water and reading lights.

Seats, parcels, whole buses — booked at the counter, on the phone, or online.

On Every Trip

  • AC sleeper and seater coaches — WiFi, blankets, reading lights.
